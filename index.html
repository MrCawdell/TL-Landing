How it works

From load posted to load delivered

01

Supplier posts a load

Specify material, tonnage, route, vehicle type and collection window. Live on the board in under 30 seconds.

02

Matched hauliers are notified instantly

Hauliers operating the right vehicle in your area receive an alert the moment your load goes live.

03

Hauliers submit a quote

Every haulier submits their rate privately. No one can see a competitor's figure. Compete on value, not a race to zero.

04

AI fills any gaps automatically

If no quotes come in, TipperLink's AI reaches out to additional hauliers automatically. No load goes unquoted.

Platform features

Built for the job. Not adapted from something else.

Every feature on TipperLink was designed around how tipper and grab haulage actually works: quarry-to-site aggregate runs, waste site clearance, walking floor biomass or agricultural movements. AI matching connects the right haulier to the right load automatically. No manual calls, no chasing. Let haulage partners come to you.

Blind quoting

Your rate is completely private. Competitors never see your quote. Quote on the value you deliver, not to undercut a visible number.

Haulier reliability scores

Completion rate, response time, and star rating appear automatically on every quote, so suppliers know who they are hiring before committing.
